About Me

IT & Software Engineer
Based in Nairobi, Kenya

I'm Nigel Muchende Kyalo — a full-stack developer and web designer with over 5 years of experience building digital products that solve real problems. My work bridges two worlds: the creative demands of international clients in the UK, and the practical needs of businesses across Kenya and East Africa.

My journey started with an Associate's Degree in Business IT from KCA University, deepened through hands-on IT support experience at Kenyatta National Hospital, and was formalised through the Power Learn Project Africa Software Development scholarship. In 2025, I earned my AI Literacy Certification from the Oterman's Institute, giving me formal expertise in AI integration, responsible deployment, and building intelligent agents for real-world business use.

I build with React, Next.js, Node.js, PHP, and modern databases including MongoDB, Supabase, and Firebase. I deploy on Vercel and Netlify, follow agile principles, and care deeply about writing clean, maintainable code. Beyond code, I mentor young creatives through Revived Artists.
